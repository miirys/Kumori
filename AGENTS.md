# Agents

Start with [AGENTS/README.md](AGENTS/README.md). It is the guide for anyone, person or agent, working on Kumori or a fork: how the app is built, how GeForce NOW behaves, the design and motion rules, the bug history, and the mistakes to avoid. Read the index, then only the topic files your task touches.

When you finish a change, update the guide with what you changed and learned, in the same format. The rules for that are at the end of AGENTS/README.md.
